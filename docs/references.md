# Sources and citations

The repository uses these sources for the methods and evidence summarized in the READMEs:

1. Burns JW et al. *Deep Learning Foundation Models for Low-Data Regimes from Classical Molecular Descriptors.* **Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling** (2026). [DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01546](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01546). CheMeleon model and pretraining method.
2. Ieremias L et al. *Minimal Structural Variation of GPR84 Full Agonist Causes Functional Switch to Inverse Agonism.* **Journal of Medicinal Chemistry** 68, 7973–8009 (2025). [DOI: 10.1021/acs.jmedchem.4c02335](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.4c02335). GPR84 functional context.
3. Suzuki S et al. *Mechanistic insight into signal bias by the agonist-dependent conformational dynamics of GPR84.* **Nature Communications** (2026). [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-75728-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75728-9). GPR84 structural context.
4. ChEMBL 37, [official data API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/) and [ChEMBL resource](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/). Experimental record and catalogue source; individual original paper DOIs remain in `data/source/` and `data/training/`.
5. Liminal Biosciences. *Aryl-triazolyl and related GPR84 antagonists and uses thereof.* [WO2024028364A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024028364A1/en), published 2024-02-08. External retrospective evaluation source; a patent, not a peer-reviewed article.
6. [RCSB PDB 9WIJ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9WIJ). Structural input for conditional pose interpretation.

For code-level third-party notices and checkpoint terms, see [THIRD_PARTY.md](../THIRD_PARTY.md). A project-level `CITATION.cff` will be added after the project authors approve its author list and release metadata; this file does not invent a project DOI or publication.
